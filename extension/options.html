Nimbl Radar extension

Sign in to connect the extension to your Radar account.

You'll sign in with your Nimbl Google account, the same as Radar. Everything you log from LinkedIn is then recorded as you.

Advanced: Radar address and manual key

Only needed if sign-in doesn't work: in Radar open Settings → Chrome extension → Create a key for this browser, then paste it here.

The extension only reads the LinkedIn page you have open and fills in text you choose. It never sends, connects or browses LinkedIn on its own: you always press Send yourself.
